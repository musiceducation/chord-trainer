Adaptation used by Chord Trainer (this directory):
- Velocity layer v80 (MIDI velocity 70–109). Clear for ear training.
- Notes C2, F#2, C3, F#3, C4, F#4, C5, F#5, C6 — every tritone, covering
  the on-screen keyboard (MIDI 36–84) with at most ±3 semitone pitch-shift.
- Each note trimmed to about 6.5 seconds with a short fade-out so the
  playback envelope (3.0 s note / 3.5 s chord) never hits a hard file end.
- Encoded from FreePats FLAC to 192 kbps AAC (.m4a) at 48 kHz.
  Sharp signs in filenames are written as "s" (F#2 → Fs2.m4a).

Product copy may read: “Electric piano samples: FreePats FM Piano 1 (CC0).”
