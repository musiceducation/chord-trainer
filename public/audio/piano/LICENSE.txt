Adaptation used by Chord Trainer (this directory):
- Velocity layer v10 only (MIDI velocity 73–80). That layer is a moderate
  mezzo-forte: clearer than a soft layer for ear training, less harsh than
  the loudest layers.
- Notes C2 through C6, sampled in minor thirds, matching the on-screen keyboard.
- Each note trimmed to 4.0 seconds with a short fade-out.
- Encoded from the original 24-bit / 48 kHz FLAC to 192 kbps AAC (.m4a)
  so the app stays small and notes start quickly. Sharp signs in filenames
  are written as "s" (D#2 → Ds2.m4a) to keep URLs simple.

Playback (not baked into the files) warms the close-miked recording:
lowshelf +4.5 dB at 250 Hz, peaking +2.5 dB at 400 Hz, peaking −4.5 dB
at 3.8 kHz, peaking −3 dB at 5.2 kHz, and highshelf −6.5 dB at 6.4 kHz.
Pitch between samples stays within one or two semitones, using the same
key ranges as the original SFZ.
