Chord Trainer piano samples
===========================

Source
------
Steinway Grand Piano factory content installed with Apple GarageBand /
Logic Pro on macOS:

  /Library/Application Support/Logic/EXS Factory Samples/
    01 Acoustic Pianos/Steinway Piano_consolidated.caf
  /Library/Application Support/Logic/Sampler Instruments/
    01 Acoustic Pianos/Steinway Grand Piano 2.exs

GarageBand’s “Steinway Grand Piano” patch loads this same EXS instrument.

What we ship
------------
Seventeen stereo AAC clips (C2–C6, every minor third), trimmed to 6.5 seconds
with a 0.5-second fade. A2 is taken from the Bb2 zone and pitch-shifted one
semitone at playback.

------------------------
