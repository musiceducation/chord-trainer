Adaptation used by Chord Trainer (this directory):
- Velocity layer v10 only (MIDI velocity 73–80). Mezzo-forte: clear for ear
  training without the harsh top layers.
- Notes C2 through C6, sampled in minor thirds, matching the on-screen keyboard.
- Each note trimmed to about 6.5 seconds (C6 keeps its natural ~6.1 s length)
  with a short fade-out so the playback envelope (3.0 s note / 3.5 s chord) never
  hits a hard file end.
- Encoded from the Salamander Ogg Vorbis pack to 192 kbps AAC (.m4a) at 48 kHz.
  Sharp signs in filenames are written as "s" (D#2 → Ds2.m4a) to keep URLs simple.
