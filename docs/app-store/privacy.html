Chord Trainer Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 4, 2026

Chord Trainer (“the App”) is a music education application. We respect your privacy.

Data We Collect

We do not collect, transmit, or sell personal data. The App does not require an account and does not use analytics, advertising, or tracking.

On-Device Storage

Practice statistics (scores, streaks, achievements, chord history, difficulty, key, and language) are stored locally on your device. This data never leaves your device unless you back up the device through Apple’s standard backup services.

In-App Purchases

The App may offer optional Support tips through Apple In-App Purchase. Payment is processed by Apple. We do not receive your card number, Apple ID password, or billing address. Tips do not unlock extra features; the App works fully without them.

Network Access

Practice works fully offline. Optional In-App Purchases contact Apple’s servers. The App does not load third-party analytics, advertising, or remote fonts.

Audio

The App synthesizes piano sounds locally with Web Audio. It does not access your microphone, camera, or location.

Children

The App is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

We may update this policy. The updated date above will change when we do.
