Chord Trainer Support

Getting Started

  1. Open the app and tap 開始練習 to enable piano audio.
  2. Choose a mode: 辨識 (recognition), 訓練 (ear training), or 統計 (stats).
  3. Use the gear icon to change difficulty.

No Sound?

  • Ensure device mute switch is off and volume is up.
  • Tap the speaker icon to confirm sound is enabled in-app.
  • Restart the app and tap 開始練習 again.

Reset Progress

Open the 統計 tab → 重設統計. This clears all local stats on your device only.
