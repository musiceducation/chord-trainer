Chord Trainer Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 12, 2026

Chord Trainer (“the App”) is a music education application published by Kenneth. We respect your privacy.

Data We Collect

We do not collect, transmit, or sell personal data. The App does not require an account.

On-Device Storage

Practice statistics (scores, streaks, achievements, and chord history) are stored locally on your device using browser storage APIs. This data never leaves your device unless you back up your device through Apple’s standard backup services.

Network Access

The App works fully offline. It does not load third-party analytics, advertising, or remote fonts.

Audio

The App synthesizes piano sounds locally. It does not access your microphone.

Children

The App is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

We may update this policy. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance.

Contact

Questions: see the Support page.

English summary: Chord Trainer stores practice progress only on your device. No data is collected or shared.
