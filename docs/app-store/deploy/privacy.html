Chord Trainer Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 6, 2026

Chord Trainer (“the App”) is a music education application. We respect your privacy.

Data We Collect

The App does not require an account. We do not sell personal data.

On-Device Storage

Practice statistics (scores, streaks, achievements, chord history, difficulty, key, and language) are stored locally on your device. This data never leaves your device unless you back up the device through Apple’s standard backup services.

Advertising

The free version shows Google AdMob banner ads on non-practice screens, including Stats, Settings, and the Pro paywall. Ads are hidden during practice rounds and while piano audio is playing. Chord Trainer Pro is ad-free.

Google may receive an advertising identifier and ad interaction data to deliver and measure ads, including personalized ads when you allow them. A consent form is shown when required. You can change this choice in iOS Settings and, when offered, Ad privacy in the app.

In-App Purchases

The App offers an optional Chord Trainer Pro monthly subscription through Apple In-App Purchase. Pro unlocks chord progressions, full stats, and an ad-free app. Payment is processed by Apple. We do not receive your card number, Apple ID password, or billing address. Optional Support tips do not unlock features.

Network Access

Practice audio stays on your device. Loading a banner ad contacts Google. Optional In-App Purchases contact Apple’s servers. The App does not require an account and does not sell personal data.

Audio

The App synthesizes piano sounds locally with Web Audio. It does not access your microphone, camera, or location.

Children

The App is suitable for general audiences and does not ask for an account. Banner ads in the free version are provided by Google and are not shown during practice.

Changes

We may update this policy. The updated date above will change when we do.
